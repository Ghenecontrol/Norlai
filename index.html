11 Roadmap

Where this goes from MVP to autonomous optimization.

Four phases, each shipping something usable — not a wall of future promises with nothing in between.

Phase
Timeline
What ships
Phase 1 — MVP
0–3 months

Crawler, AI audit, conversion scoring, recommendations, basic dashboard, auth, monitoring. Goal: validate whether companies act on AI-generated recommendations.

Phase 2 — Intelligence
3–6 months

Behaviour analytics, visitor intent detection, AI content optimizer, lead intelligence, competitor analysis, automated alerts. Goal: move from "audit my website" to "understand my website."

Phase 3 — Optimization
6–9 months

A/B testing, AI experiment recommendations, landing page generator, conversion forecasting, CRM integrations. Goal: move from understand to improve.

Phase 4 — Autonomous
9–12+ months

AI detects an opportunity, proposes a change, runs the experiment, measures the outcome, and learns for next time — with a human able to review at every step.
